## 5. What to expect next trading day

**HY-specific**
- **Tone likely to stay constructive but headline-sensitive.** Positive flows, solid technicals, and
  supportive fundamentals argue for spreads holding near the tighter end of the recent range (~260–275 bps),

- **Dispersion, not direction, is the trade:** up-in-quality (BB over CCC), watch fallen-angel migration

**Macro events (key catalyst)**
- **US CPI — Wednesday, Aug 12, 8:30 AM ET (July data).** Consensus ~**2.8–3.4% headline YoY** and
  ~**3.0% core**; core MoM ~0.24%. This is *the* swing factor: a hot print (compounded by firm PPI later in
  the week) would revive September rate-hike odds under a hawkish-leaning Fed and pressure spreads/duration;
  an in-line-to-soft print extends the rally.
- **PPI later in the week** and any Fed-speak for confirmation of the dovish repricing.

**Geopolitical / other risks**
- **Middle East / oil.** The Iran conflict (Hormuz strikes, broken ceasefire, Houthi targeting of Saudi
  tankers) keeps an oil-price/inflation tail risk live — supportive for energy HY but an upside inflation
  threat that could undercut the rate-cut narrative. Conversely, any US–Iran de-escalation could sharply
  correct crude lower.
- **Trade policy.** The 145% China tariff plus newly announced rounds (Canada 50%, Brazil 25%, 60+ partners
  10–12%) continue to pressure import-reliant, levered HY issuers' margins and guidance.

**Bottom line for the desk:** stay constructive but keep powder dry into Wednesday's CPI; favor quality and
carry, watch energy for the oil bid, and keep a close eye on fallen-angel and tariff-exposed names for
idiosyncratic widening.
